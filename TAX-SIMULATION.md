Regional settings are game approximations in USD, not a tax filing calculator. Dubai is selectable as a city in the United Arab Emirates. The Middle East roster includes the Gulf states, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Iraq, Iran, Israel, Palestine, Yemen, Turkey and Cyprus; Egypt is already available.

UAE salary income has no personal income tax. Company profit uses 9% above a fixed game conversion of AED 375,000 ($102,110). Oman salary tax switches on in game year 2028 at 5% above a fixed game conversion of OMR 42,000 ($109,200). Other regional income bands, living costs and corporate rates are simplified presets; nationality, residency, social contributions, special industries and exemptions are not simulated. Do not interpret these presets as exact real-world rates.
